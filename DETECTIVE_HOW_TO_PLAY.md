# eDNA Detectives

Compare past and present eDNA surveys, test possible causes, and solve a seamount mystery with EDNA.

## Start

Open `game` in Unity **6000.4.6f1**, open `Assets/Scenes/InvestigationScene.unity`, then press **Play**.

## How to play

### 1. Observe

1. Begin with **Today** and click **Start recording** to collect the survey records.
2. Move the slider to **20 years ago** and record the historical survey.
3. Compare each species in the notebook. Drag it into **More**, **Fewer**, **Not detected**, or **Same**, or click the species and then its change.
4. Once all six findings are recorded, save your summary and continue to **Investigate**.

### 2. Investigate

1. Click **Play** on each of the three required scenario cards.
2. Watch the groups change and compare the predictions with your notebook. Use **Replay** to watch again.
3. After trying the three required models, click **Review this explanation** on the model you want to investigate.
4. Use EDNA’s **Compare again** button to return to the models and review the other fishing explanation. After reviewing both the **trawling model** and **long-line alternative**, click **Record conclusion** to finish. The summary shows **Reviewed** or **Awaiting review** as status labels.

**Optional:** play **Toxic algal bloom** to explore an extreme scenario and compare it with the survey. It is not required to complete the case.

## Helpful controls

- **EDNA:** follow her highlighted guidance; use **Show me where** for help in Investigate.
- **Details:** hover over scenario titles, species names, results or survey records. Click/tap to keep details open; a playing prediction pauses until you close them.
- **Notebook icon:** open or close your saved records.
- **Restart icon** at the top right: start a new investigation after confirming.
- **Investigate again:** replay the Detective scene after recording your conclusion.
